Key IRS Tax Deadlines You Should Know

Managing your deadlines is critical to avoiding penalties and ensuring compliance. Here are the key dates every business should track:

January

Deadline to submit

Deadline to submit prior-year documents and issue Form 1099 to contractors.

March 15

LLC & S-Corp Tax Filing Deadline

Corporate Income Tax filing for LLCs (Form 1065) and S-Corps. Also the last day to request an extension for these entities.

April 15

C-Corp & Personal Tax Filing Deadline

Filing deadline for C-Corps and personal tax returns. Also the deadline to file extensions.

May 1

Florida Annual Report Due

All Florida business entities—including LLCs and corporations—must file their Annual Report with the state by this deadline to maintain active status and avoid penalties.

October 15

Final Extended Tax Deadline

October 15 is the final day to file extended federal tax returns for C-Corporations and individuals. After this date, penalties may apply for late filings.

*Note: State deadlines may vary. We help ensure you're up to date across all jurisdictions.
